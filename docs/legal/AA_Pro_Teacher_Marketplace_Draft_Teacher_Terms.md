# AA Pro Teacher Marketplace — Draft Teacher Terms

> **WORKING DRAFT — NOT APPROVED, NOT IN FORCE, NOT LEGAL ADVICE.**
> Prepared by Claude for the owner's review (OC-79 Part D, 16 Oct 2026). It needs review by a qualified lawyer before launch. It is not shown to teachers, and no one is asked to accept it. Text in **[square brackets]** marks a point a lawyer or the owner must decide.

**Version:** draft 0.1 · 16 Oct 2026
**Applies to:** the Teacher Marketplace part of AA Pro, which covers "Looking for Students" listings, the teacher directory and enquiries between parents and teachers.
**Relationship to other terms:** these terms add to the general AA Pro teacher terms (draft, OC-71), which still cover the teacher account, approval and teaching through AA Pro. **[Lawyer: decide whether to merge the two documents, and which one prevails if they conflict.]**

## 1. What the marketplace is

1.1 The marketplace helps parents and approved teachers **find each other**. AA Pro shows teacher listings and passes enquiries between parents and teachers.

1.2 Discovery is separate from what comes next. An enquiry, an accepted enquiry or a listing is **not** a lesson, a booking, a contract to teach or a payment. Lessons, prices and payments are agreed later, between the parent and the teacher, using the steps described in section 7 and section 9.

## 2. Who can take part

2.1 Only teachers who have completed AA Pro's onboarding and been **approved by AA Pro** may publish a listing or receive marketplace enquiries.

2.2 Teachers whose account is pending, rejected, suspended or deactivated cannot publish listings, appear in the directory or receive enquiries. If an account is suspended or deactivated, its listing is hidden immediately.

2.3 **Approval is not a guarantee.** AA Pro's approval means the teacher passed AA Pro's onboarding checks at that time. It does not mean AA Pro guarantees the teacher's qualifications, experience, suitability, conduct or teaching results. **[Owner and lawyer: describe exactly what the approval check covers, so this sentence stays accurate.]**

## 3. Accurate profile and listing information

3.1 Teachers must keep their profile and listing accurate and up to date. This covers their approved subjects and grades, curriculum, experience, availability, teaching mode and asking prices.

3.2 A teacher may list only the **subjects and grades approved on their AA Pro profile**. AA Pro's systems refuse others. To add a subject or grade, the teacher changes their profile, and AA Pro reviews the change first.

3.3 Experience, biography and other claims are shown **"as stated by the teacher"** unless AA Pro has separately verified them and says so. AA Pro does not describe a claim as verified or certified without a real verification process.

3.4 Listings must not contain contact details, links to other ways of contacting the teacher, or misleading, offensive or unlawful content.

## 4. Listings and availability

4.1 Before a listing is published, the teacher sees exactly which details will be public, and agrees to them being shown. Public details are: name, approved subjects and grades, curriculum, experience (as stated), online teaching and asking prices. They never include email, phone number, students or children's information.

4.2 A teacher can pause or withdraw their listing at any time. Paused, withdrawn, expired and hidden listings do not appear in search or on public pages.

4.3 A published listing **expires after 60 days** unless the teacher renews it. AA Pro aims to remind the teacher **7 days before** expiry. Until real email delivery is set up, the reminder is shown inside AA Pro. When renewing, the teacher confirms their availability, prices and approved subjects are still correct.

4.4 Availability shown on a listing is **a guide only**. It does not reserve any time and is not a confirmed booking.

4.5 Teaching through the marketplace is **online only** for now. In-person lessons are not offered.

## 5. Enquiries

5.1 An enquiry is a parent's **expression of interest**. It is not a confirmed lesson, contract, booking or payment.

5.2 A teacher may accept or decline an enquiry, with an optional note. Unanswered enquiries expire after 14 days.

5.3 Accepting an enquiry **does not**:
- link the teacher to the child;
- give the teacher access to the child's profile, books or learning information;
- reserve a lesson time; or
- fix a price.

5.4 After acceptance, the parent decides whether to add the teacher for their child using AA Pro's normal linking steps. They also choose which subjects the teacher may see. Any lesson then follows AA Pro's booking process when it is available.

5.5 AA Pro may limit how many enquiries or reports an account can send in a period, to prevent spam. These limits can change.

## 6. Children's safety and privacy

6.1 Teachers must respect children's privacy and follow applicable safeguarding requirements. They must use any child-related information only for the authorised educational purpose. **[Lawyer: name the safeguarding and child-data laws that apply in each country where AA Pro operates, for example India and Singapore.]**

6.2 Before a parent links the teacher, an enquiry shows only:
- the parent's first name;
- the child's grade and curriculum, and the subjects;
- the preferred times, teaching mode and the parent's message.

It **never** shows the child's name or learning history.

6.3 Teachers must not:
- try to get around AA Pro's access controls;
- try to obtain information about families who have not contacted them or linked them;
- ask for children's personal details beyond what the lesson needs; or
- use AA Pro for harassment, spam, advertising unrelated services or any inappropriate communication.

6.4 **Reporting concerns.** Parents and teachers can report a listing or a message from within AA Pro. The person reported is not told who reported them. AA Pro staff authorised to handle reports look into them. **[Owner: confirm who handles reports (Super Admin, or an Admin with a moderation permission) and the expected response time.]**

6.5 After looking into a concern, AA Pro may:
- ask for changes;
- hide, pause or remove a listing;
- restrict enquiries; or
- suspend or deactivate an account.

Where a child may be at risk, AA Pro may also act straight away and contact the appropriate authorities where the law requires or allows it. **[Lawyer: confirm reporting duties and wording.]**

## 7. Contact details

7.1 Contact details are **never public**. They are not shown in listings, search results or pending enquiries.

7.2 Contact details may be exchanged only **after an enquiry is accepted and when both the parent and the teacher choose to share them**. Accepting an enquiry does not by itself allow any personal information to be disclosed. Either person can withdraw their agreement before the details are shown. Once shown, AA Pro cannot take details back from the other person.

7.3 Contact details received this way may be used only to arrange lessons for that family. They must not be shared with anyone else or used for marketing.

## 8. Prices and payments

8.1 Prices on a listing are **asking prices**. They are not guaranteed final charges. A listing may show up to three prices (hourly, weekly, every two weeks or monthly) in INR, SGD or USD. AA Pro does not convert between currencies.

8.2 **Parents and teachers arrange payment directly between themselves.** In the initial marketplace AA Pro does not collect, hold, transfer, refund or guarantee any payment. There is no buyer or seller protection, and AA Pro does not take a commission.

8.3 AA Pro does not promise that a listing or enquiry will lead to students, lessons or income.

8.4 Any future payment service through AA Pro will need separate approval by AA Pro's owner and separate terms.

8.5 Teachers are responsible for their own tax, registration and invoicing obligations on fees they receive. **[Lawyer: confirm.]**

## 9. Lessons after a match

9.1 Once real booking is available, the parent and teacher agree the schedule, teaching mode, duration and price through AA Pro's booking process. The agreed price is recorded separately from the listing's asking price.

9.2 Human lessons are currently limited to 40 minutes each, unless AA Pro changes this rule.

## 10. Moderation and account status

10.1 AA Pro may review listings and reported content. It may restrict, hide, pause or remove content that breaks these terms or AA Pro's rules, under the final approved moderation policy. **[Owner: approve the moderation policy, including how a teacher is told and how they can ask for a review of a decision.]**

10.2 AA Pro may suspend or deactivate a teacher who breaks these terms. Suspended, rejected or deactivated teachers cannot publish listings or use restricted marketplace features.
