The Four Rocky Neighbors

Our Sun sits at the gravitational center of the solar system. The four innermost bodies closest to the Sun are known as the Terrestrial Planets because they possess solid, rocky crusts, metallic cores, and relatively dense atmospheres.

Planetary Surface Characteristics

draw

Mars is commonly referred to as the Red Planet due to iron oxide (rust) widespread across its surface regolith. Despite being colder than Earth with an average temperature of -80°F, Mars contains the largest extinct shield volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons.

Between Mars and Jupiter lies the Asteroid Belt, forming the dividing barrier between these terrestrial inner worlds and the massive gas giants.

lightbulb
Did You Know? Curious Fact

Venus is Hotter Than Mercury!

Even though Mercury orbits closest to the Sun, Venus holds the title for the hottest surface temperature in the entire solar system (around 465°C / 870°F)! Its dense, heavy blanket of carbon dioxide clouds traps heat in a runaway greenhouse effect!
