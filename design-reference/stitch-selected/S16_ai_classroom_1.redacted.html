The Inner Planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars)

orbit Fig 4.1: The Terrestrial Planets Orbit SchematicInteractive Figure

👉 Tap a planet above to inspect its distance

The four inner planets closest to the Sun are composed mostly of rock and metal. Mars is commonly referred to as the Red Planet because of plentiful iron oxide minerals (rust) across its dusty surface. This rust floats into Mars’ thin atmosphere, scattering light and creating its distinctive crimson sky.

Did You Know?

A single day on Venus (one full rotation on its axis) is longer than a Venusian year! It takes Venus 243 Earth days to rotate once, but only 225 Earth days to complete one orbit around the Sun.

Inner planetary composition: Dense silicate crusts and metallic cores.
