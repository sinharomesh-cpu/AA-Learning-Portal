## Brand & Style
The design system positions learning as an expansive, luminous voyage of discovery. The aesthetic targets ambitious learners, students, and educators seeking a premium, immersive platform that feels both technologically cutting-edge and academically rigorous. The experience transitions intentionally: from a grand, cinematic cosmic atmosphere in the hero environment to an impeccably clean, highly legible, structured pedagogical space across the core learning flows.

The design language balances **Cinematic Glassmorphism** and **Refined Contemporary SaaS Minimalism**:
- **Cosmic Depth:** Atmospheric, dark-canvas surfaces accented by ethereal aurora glows, celestial light bleeds, and radiant rim illumination.
- **Pedagogical Clarity:** High-contrast, scannable structures that make curricula, AI-tutor dialogues, and textbook integrations feel frictionless and inviting.
- **Luminous Precision:** Subtly glowing vector strokes, translucent frosted overlays, and pristine typography evoke advanced scientific exploration without leaning into gimmicky sci-fi tropes.

## Colors
The system employs an intentional duality: a nocturnal planetary spectrum for macro exploration and atmospheric immersion, balanced with high-efficiency light-receptive zones for deep study.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#45D6E8` - Electric Cyan):** Designates interactive clarity, active states, key data signals, and luminous focal points across dark surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#006194` - AA Pro Blue):** Anchors brand authority, structured UI foundations, robust card frames, and core functional headers.
- **Tertiary (`#8B70FF` - Aurora Violet):** Enriches visual transitions, AI teacher indicators, interactive highlights, and celestial ambient gradients.
- **Accent Warning/Reward (`#FFB454` - Solar Amber):** Reserved for achievements, mastery badges, critical notifications, and solar energetic highlights.
- **Base Backgrounds:**
  - Deep Midnight Navy (`#0B1026`): Canvas base for cinematic and hero segments.
  - Deep Space Blue (`#071A38`): Mid-ground containment and deep interactive modules.
  - Surface Glass: Translucent variations (`rgba(11, 16, 38, 0.72)`) backed by subtle optical refraction.
- **Educational Clean Surface Mode:** Transitions below the cosmic fold into pure Arctic White (`#FFFFFF`) and Celestial Ice Tint (`#F4F7FC`), bordered with subtle slate tones (`#DCE5F2`) and deep carbon text (`#071A38`) to ensure effortless, sustained reading comprehension.

## Typography
The typographic balance relies on the geometric, optimistic cadence of **Outfit** for structural titles and wayfinding, paired with the pristine mechanical legibility of **Inter** for sustained instructional content.

### Hierarchy & Rules
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Rendered in Outfit with tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to produce impactful, cohesive lockups over cosmic imagery.
- **Body & Content Blocks:** Handled strictly by Inter. Generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensure reading ease across textbook snippets, AI interaction dialogs, and curriculum tables.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Outfit in medium to bold weights with uppercase tracking applied to tags, category badges, and system statuses.
- **Contrast Ratios:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and Ice White (`#EDF4FF`) on midnight cosmic backdrops provide a minimum 12:1 contrast ratio. In light pedagogical sections, deep charcoal navy (`#071A38`) guarantees 14:1 contrast against clean white canvas layers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a 12-column responsive fluid grid structured to scale gracefully from massive, borderless visual canvases down to compact handheld formats.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, max content width bounded at `1360px` with `4rem` outer canvas padding.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `2rem` outer page margins.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` outer page margins.

### Section Flow & Reflow Principles
- **Hero & Cinematic Zones:** Utilize expansive vertical breathing space (`space-xl` multiplied for rhythm) to allow planetary backdrops and glow fields room to breathe.
- **Learning & Matrix Sections:** Transition into compact, organized multi-column card arrays that collapse from 3- or 4-column desktop grids (e.g., Subjects, Meet Teachers) into 2-column tablet setups and stacked single-column mobile arrangements.
- **Edge Containment:** All content layers respect fluid edge boundaries while ambient background cosmic light layers bleeds freely to viewport edges.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is articulated through luminous stratification, atmospheric light scatter, and frosted glass refractions rather than traditional muddy drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiers
1. **Tier 0 (Deep Space Base):** Solid Midnight Navy (`#0B1026`) layered with dynamic CSS radial gradients containing Electric Cyan and Aurora Violet glows at `8%` to `15%` opacity.
2. **Tier 1 (Subsurface Glass Plates):** Deep Space Blue (`#071A38`) fill at `65%` alpha, backed by a `16px` to `24px` backdrop blur, framed with a 1px border colored `rgba(69, 214, 232, 0.18)`.
3. **Tier 2 (Floating Action Panels & Highlights):** Higher translucency (`80%`) with localized inner highlights (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`) and external luminescent shadows: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(7, 26, 56, 0.65), 0 0 24px -2px rgba(69, 214, 232, 0.22)`.
4. **Pedagogical Light-Mode Elevation:** Light-mode cards utilize ultra-crisp `1px` structural borders (`#E2E8F0`) reinforced by ultra-soft, diffused ambient elevation: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(7, 26, 56, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(7, 26, 56, 0.04)`.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy emphasizes circular, aerodynamic, and orbital geometric forms:
- **Pill Forms (`9999px`):** Used universally across interactive buttons, system badges, search containers, and status chips to convey orbital smoothness.
- **Panels and Modular Cards (`rounded-lg` - 2rem / 32px):** Feature pronounced curved corners that mimic visor interfaces and modern hardware screens.
- **Interactive Micro-controls (`rounded-md` - 1rem / 16px):** Ensure inputs, toggle switches, and list units align cleanly with the overall curvature without feeling bulbous.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background is a vibrant linear flow of Electric Cyan (`#45D6E8`) fading softly to AA Pro Blue (`#006194`). Text is pure white Outfit semi-bold. Border: 1px translucent cyan rim (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`). Hover triggers an outer cyan aura blur (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(69, 214, 232, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary Glass Pill:** Frosted semi-transparent surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), backdrop blur `12px`, with a crisp 1px border (`rgba(69, 214, 232, 0.3)`). Hover shifts background to `rgba(69, 214, 232, 0.15)` with pure white text.
- **Solar Action Pill:** Reserved for urgent milestones or checkout triggers: Solar Amber (`#FFB454`) solid base with Deep Space Blue text (`#071A38`) for uncompromised contrast.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped tags (`rounded-full`) with subtle tint backgrounds (`rgba(139, 112, 255, 0.12)` for AI markers, `rgba(69, 214, 232, 0.12)` for core subjects).
- Bounded by a micro-border of matching hue at `35%` opacity, paired with an icon or glowing radial status dot.

### Cards
- **Cosmic Hero Cards:** Translucent dark space surfaces with gradient perimeter strokes. Top border features a distinct 1px bright neon hairline fading to zero on the sides.
- **Educational Clean Cards (Subjects & How It Works):** Crisp Arctic White backgrounds, 1.5rem padding, subtle slate outline (`#E5ECF6`). On hover, the border illuminates in Electric Cyan accompanied by an upward `4px` translation.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Elongated pill-shaped search bars with deep navy glass backdrop (`rgba(7, 26, 56, 0.7)`), 1.5px glowing rim on focus, and an integrated Electric Cyan circular action button inset at the right edge.
- Placeholder text in muted celestial blue (`#7A8EAD`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Custom circular and squircle geometries utilizing electric outlines. Checked state fills with Electric Cyan or Aurora Violet displaying a crisp white central mark.

### Specialized System Modules
- **AI Teacher Interaction Bubble:** Frosted Aurora Violet tint with a continuous, gentle pulsating outer aura to indicate real-time intelligence.
- **Book Upload / Reader Pane:** High-clarity, high-contrast module with dual-page clean formatting, crisp slate borders, and an Electric Cyan reading progress tracker running along the perimeter.
