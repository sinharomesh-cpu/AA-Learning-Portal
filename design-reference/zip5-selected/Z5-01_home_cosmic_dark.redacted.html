HOW IT WORKS

Three simple paths

Everything you need for a seamless, protected learning experience tailored for each member of the family.

For parents
1

Create your account and add each child. You choose their username and password.

2

Pick their subjects and set quiet times when lessons can't be booked.

3

Book AI lessons, add your child's own teacher or find one here, and see what your child learned.

For students
1

Sign in with the username your parent gave you.

2

Book an AI lesson at a time that's open for you.

3

Talk with the AI Tutor while your book is on the screen.

For teachers
1

Register yourself, or accept the invitation when a parent adds you.

2

The AA Pro team checks and approves your profile.

3

Set your available times and teach your students on Zoom.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents and students need to know about safety, pricing cadences, and dual-mode learning.

In strict accordance with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and our ChildSafe 2.4 framework, direct personal contact details (cell numbers, WhatsApps, personal emails, residential addresses) of both educators and student families are completely protected.

All session bookings, schedule adjustments, homework submissions, and Zoom link deliveries are orchestrated securely through your parent dashboard. This eliminates spam, protects children from unauthorized private communications, and creates an audited, worry-free environment.

Human Teacher Mode gives your child live, face-to-face 1:1 or small group guidance with accredited subject specialists (e.g., Cambridge, Singapore Math).

AI Class Mode (powered by AI Teacher) is available 24/7 between your human lessons. It helps your child practice textbook exercises, explains challenging concepts with gentle Socratic hints, and quizzes them before tests—without parents needing to teach late at night.

We believe in total pricing transparency with four flexible cadences:

  • Hourly / Single: Ideal for one-off doubt clearing or pre-exam revision.
  • Weekly (2 sessions): Great for steady homework support.
  • Bi-Weekly (4 sessions): Structured module pacing.
  • Monthly (8 sessions): Comprehensive deep-dive learning packs with up to 20% discount.

Unused sessions roll over automatically if rescheduled at least 6 hours in advance.
